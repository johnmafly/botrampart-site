What Express does

  • Install

    Nine frameworks, one prompt.

    Express, Fastify, Next.js, Django, FastAPI, Rails, Laravel, Spring Boot and Go, each with its own mount point and layout file.

  • Provision

    Infrastructure as code.

    Core, the bucket and scoped credentials on Cloudflare, AWS, Google Cloud, Azure or your Kubernetes, written as Terraform, Pulumi or one CLI script.

  • Edge

    The CDN you already use.

    Cloudflare Worker, CloudFront Lambda@Edge or the nginx module, sharing one session budget with the app.

  • Verify

    Checked before you enforce.

    Your agent proves headers, the proof-of-work and the decision log work, then stops and reports.

What your agent does

Six steps. You approve the two that matter.

  1. 01

    Site keys

    Generates br_keys locally and stores it in your secret store by name.

  2. 02

    Provision

    Writes Core, the bucket and a scoped lake_writer credential as code, shows you the plan and waits for your yes.

  3. 03

    Install

    Adds the backend SDK for your framework ahead of every route and the browser script to your layout.

  4. 04

    Edge

    Deploys the adapter for your CDN on the same keys and Core, so budgets are shared.

  5. 05

    Verify

    Checks BR-Decision headers, /_br/status, the decision log and one decision lookup.

  6. 06

    Stop before enforce

    Reports what changed and waits. Enforcing stays your call, after a policy replay.
